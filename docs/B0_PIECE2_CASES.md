# B0 piece 2: cases of the PDG comparison to check

Generated by `scripts/tools/pdg_review_cases.py`; do not edit by hand. Decisions and their
reasons go into `docs/B0_PIECE2_REVIEW.md`, by case number.

A case is one PDG final-state entry for which the comparison with the decay file is not
clean: a line that takes its c from this entry carries a warning flag, or the decay file
still disagrees with the entry by more than 3σ after all c are applied ("not closed").
Cases are ordered by the largest share of the exclusive yield of their lines in any of the 72
sample tables. 18 cases have a share of at least 0.05 % and are written in full; the other
33 are in the table at the end.

**Reading a case.** "PDG" is the measured value of the entry. "MC sum" is what the decay file
gives for the same final state, adding all lines that produce it. "after c" is the same sum
with every line multiplied by its c. In the line table: BF = rate in the decay file; P = the
fraction of the line that ends in this final state; how / PDG id = where the line takes its c
from (exact = an entry for exactly this line, product = an entry B × B(sub-decay), node = a
multi-body entry, empty = no c); "this entry" marks the lines whose c comes from this entry;
share = largest share of the exclusive yield in any sample table.
